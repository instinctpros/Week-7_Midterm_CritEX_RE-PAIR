About this guide +

Repair means tending to your relationship with attention, choice, and kinship. Small actions do not resolve every structural problem. These are reflective invitations, not assessments or diagnoses. You can stop whenever you need to.

Your answers stay in this page. The application does not send or save them. Refreshing clears this session.

Illustrations are editable SVG placeholders. The branching background is a visual metaphor, not an anatomical diagram. In/Pivot’s visual language informs the arrows, warmth, and invitation to redesign a life.

Sources and interview excerpts +

Excerpts from interviews with Kristen McGuiness and Alexander Williams Tolbert are from 2026 interview transcripts. The excerpts are personal perspectives, not empirical findings. The other prompts are original reflective exercises.

Source files / p5.js / print +

This workbook uses HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and SVG. The p5.js export mounts the same interface without a canvas. It needs no external assets.
